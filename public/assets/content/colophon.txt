This page is heavily inspired by the work of the great
https://github.com/remojansen Using his great `cool-retro-term-webgl` project
(https://github.com/remojansen/cool-retro-term-webgl), which is in turn a port
to WebGL from `cool-retro-term` (https://github.com/Swordfish90/cool-retro-term)
by Filippo Scognamiglio. 

I have extended this work with colour ANSI support for the terminal as well as a
mock-BBS UI, as well as some asset optimisations and integration into an Astro
build and Cloudflare Worker deploy (my preferred light-weight stack).

I've used OpenAI Codex with GPT-5.5 (Medium Intelligence setting) throughout
development as part of my personal learning of how to integrate AI Agents into
my creative workflow.
